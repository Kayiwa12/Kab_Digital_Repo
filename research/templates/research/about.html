About the Research Discovery & Analytics Platform

Architectural principles, DSpace REST API integration, and mathematical text similarity methodology.

1. Institutional Context & Purpose

Kabale University's Institutional Digital Repository (IDR), powered by DSpace, preserves and disseminates the scholarly output of the university, including postgraduate dissertations, faculty publications, and institutional conference proceedings.

This platform serves as an Enhanced Discovery and Bibliometric Layer deployed atop the repository. By extracting, normalizing, and indexing metadata via DSpace’s official REST API, the platform provides advanced multifaceted search, historical trend analytics, author portfolios, and mathematical similarity matching without altering the authoritative preservation role of DSpace.

2. Relationship with Kabale University DSpace

DSpace Remains the Single Source of Truth

This platform does not host independent files or duplicate full-text documents. Every research item displays permanent handles, official DSpace UUIDs, and direct hyperlinks back to the primary repository record on idr.kab.ac.ug.

The platform queries DSpace 9.x HAL+JSON endpoints, specifically:

  • /server/api/discover/search/objects: Paginated discovery search
  • /server/api/core/communities/{uuid}: Target Postgraduate Community
  • /server/api/core/collections/{uuid}: Faculty & institute collections

3. Related Research Methodology (TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity)

Unlike systems that rely on opaque generative AI models, our Related Research Engine uses conventional, peer-reviewed computational text analysis:

TF-IDF Vectorization

Evaluates Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency across concatenated titles, abstracts, subject keywords, and academic faculties. Common English and institutional stopwords are removed to isolate high-information conceptual descriptors.

Cosine Similarity Metric

Measures the cosine of the angle between high-dimensional document vectors in Euclidean space. A score of 1.0 represents vector identity, while scores closer to 0 represent orthogonal topical profiles.

Academic Caveat: Textual similarity measures vocabulary overlap and shared subject classification; it does not infer identical findings, academic agreement, or methodological equivalence.

4. Research Coverage Analysis & Caveats

The Research Coverage module measures relative topic representation only across records indexed in the Kabale University repository.

If an area is designated as "Low Repository Coverage," this indicates that few postgraduate theses in the indexed collection have focused on that topic. It must never be interpreted as a claim that no scientific research exists in the wider global academic literature.
